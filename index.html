A letter, just for you

Dear Jessa Mae,

I've been trying to find the right words, and I keep landing on this one: you make things feel lighter. Not in a loud way — in that quiet way only certain people can.

Your laugh is my favourite kind of interruption. You have this way of turning a long day into a short one, and a heavy one into something I can actually carry. I don't know how you do it. I'm just glad you do.

I still think about our prayers sometimes — those small, ordinary minutes when the world went quiet and it was just us and God. Thank you for always making the time. You'll never know how much those minutes held me together.

Some people get lucky in money. Some get lucky in love. I think I got lucky in people — and of all the people, I got you. Kind. Funny. Honest. Real.

So here's my wish for you: may your days be gentle, your coffee always warm, and your heart stay exactly as soft as it is right now.

With all my heart, Muwonge Derrick Lubega
